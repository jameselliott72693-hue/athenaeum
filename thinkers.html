    Agreement matrix

    A shared sitting is a night where both were seated. When that sitting is listed in the agreement file, it counts as an agreement. When the split on that sitting puts them on different sides, it counts as a disagreement.

    • Agreed, no split
    • Agreed, and split on some
    • Split on every shared agreement
    • Seated together, no agreement on the record

    Quotation marks appear only around a verified passage from a sitting. A note from the shelf is the shelf's own wording. An agreement count is a sitting listed in the agreement file. Nothing on this page adds a life, a line, or a tally that the files do not already hold.
